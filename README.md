## About Me
> [GoogleScholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=o-TtUq0AAAAJ&hl=en) &nbsp; · &nbsp; [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/dillanimans/) &nbsp; · &nbsp; [CV](DillanImansCV.pdf)

I'm a computer vision researcher at [CAMP TUM](https://www.cs.cit.tum.de/camp/start/) and M.Sc. student in the Elite Master's Program AI in Biomedicine at TU Munich. My work spans multimodal perception for surgical and robotic settings (scene understanding, world modeling from surgical video, self-supervised representations) and medical image analysis (segmentation, domain adaptation, cross-modal learning for MRI, CT, fundus). I'm increasingly focused on perception and world models for autonomous robots, building hands-on robotics projects in C++ and ROS 2.

Reach out, I'd love to talk: dillanimansbusiness@gmail.com
